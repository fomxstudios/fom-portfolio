01

Services

FOM Studios provides photography and videography services including shooting, editing and delivery of final media.

02

Booking & Payment Terms

  • 50% non-refundable deposit required.
  • Remaining balance payable before the shoot date.
  • No services rendered without full payment.
  • Payment confirms acceptance of this agreement.
03

Cancellation Policy

  • Deposit is non-refundable.
  • Cancellations more than 10 days before the shoot incur no additional payment.
  • Cancellations less than 10 days before the shoot incur an additional fee equal to 20% of the outstanding balance.
  • Cancellations must be submitted in writing.
04

Rescheduling

  • One reschedule permitted per booking.
  • Payments transfer to the new date.
  • Subject to availability.
05

Content Delivery

  • Delivery timelines communicated before booking.
  • Rush delivery incurs a 20% fee.
  • Raw files are not included unless agreed.
06

Creative Control & Usage Rights

  • FOM Studios retains creative control.
  • Content may be used for portfolio and marketing purposes unless confidentiality is agreed in writing.
07

Liability

  • FOM Studios is not liable for circumstances beyond reasonable control.
  • Force majeure provisions apply.
